Configure your roster, generate a lineup, and download a PDF for the coaches.

How To Generate A Lineup
  1. Mark who's Attending Game and fill in each player's ratings below (optional, but improves batting order and bias results).
  2. Set each player's Pitcher Availability (Innings), and lock in any Pitcher Innings or Catcher Innings you already know.
  3. Use Excluded Positions to exclude positions a player shouldn't play, and turn on any bias checkboxes you want applied.
  4. Click Save Roster Settings so your changes stick around next time you open the app.
  5. Click Generate lineup — it jumps to the Lineup Card tab, where you can still drag-and-drop to adjust the batting order or override any position, then download a PDF.
What Export / Import Roster Settings to Share do

Your roster settings are saved only in this browser — it doesn't travel with the app file itself. Export downloads a small settings file with everything you've entered (attendance, ratings, pitcher/catcher innings, positions, biases) so you can send it to a co-coach along with the app. They click Import and select that file to load your exact roster settings into their own copy of the app.

Glenview Fall League Rules (11U)

Condensed from the official 2026 Glenview Fall League Rules, focused on what applies to an 11U team and to building a lineup card.

⚾ Pitching (11U)
  • Pitchers may pitch no more than 3 innings per game. Pitching any part of an inning counts as a full inning. The Pitcher Availability (Innings) dropdown caps at 3.
  • A pitcher who is removed cannot re-enter the pitching spot later in the game — in practice this means a 2-inning pitcher's two innings must be back-to-back. The app requires pitcher innings to be consecutive.
  • Relief pitchers get no more than 5 warm-up pitches and should warm up in dead-ball territory, not on the mound before the game.
  • No curveballs, sliders, forkballs, or any pitch relying on an arm-twisting elbow motion (fastballs, change-ups, and knuckleballs are fine). Umpire's judgment call.
  • One balk warning per pitcher per game.
  • A pitcher who hits 3 batters in a game must be removed from the pitching spot for the rest of that game.
  • Coaches get one free mound visit per inning; a second visit in the same inning requires removing the pitcher.
🥅 Batting & Baserunning
  • Teams must bat the entire roster continuously; free defensive substitutions are allowed. Every attending player is included in the batting order.
  • Late-arriving players bat at the bottom of the order. Not automated — after generating, drag that player to the bottom of the batting order manually.
  • Players called up from a lower division (when short of 9 registered players) cannot pitch or catch and must bat at the bottom of the order. This app doesn't currently model guest call-up players separately from your roster — let me know if you'd like that added.
  • An injured player's spot is skipped without penalty when their turn comes up; an ejected player's spot becomes an automatic out.
  • Courtesy runners are allowed for catchers, pitchers, and injured players (mandatory for the catcher with two outs). The last out — not the last batted out — runs.
  • Full baseball rules apply at 11U, including leadoffs, infield fly rule, balks, and dropped third strike. Stealing home is allowed.
👥 Roster & Game Settings
  • Team rosters may have up to 15 players submitted on SportsEngine before the first game.
  • 8 players is the minimum to avoid a forfeit. The app flags a forfeit-risk warning if fewer than 8 are marked attending.
  • No more than 3 coaches and 1 scorekeeper on the bench.
  • Metal spikes are not allowed at 11U. All players must wear a protective cup; catchers need full protective gear (team-provided, not league-provided). Helmets required for batters, on-deck batters, base runners, and base coaches.
⏱️ Game Length & Field
  • Games are 7 innings, official once the losing team has batted 4 times. The app is built around a 7-inning lineup card.
  • No new inning begins after 1 hour 45 minutes.
  • Mercy rule: 15 runs after 3 innings, 10 after 4, 8 after 5.
  • Bases are 70 feet apart; pitching distance is 48 feet.

This section is a summary for quick reference — always defer to the official Glenview Fall League Rules document for anything not covered here or in case of any discrepancy.

Lineup Generation Rules

⚾ Pitching
  • Pitcher Availability (Innings) (0, 1, 2, or 3) sets the maximum innings a player may pitch. Set to 0 to prevent pitching entirely.
  • Pitcher Innings buttons (1–7) lock a pitcher into specific innings (highlighted blue). Innings must be consecutive and you cannot select more than the player's availability allows. Two pitchers cannot be assigned the same inning — the app blocks this at selection time.
  • Pitchers with innings assigned are locked — the algorithm pre-slots them before anything else runs.
  • Pitchers with availability > 0 but no innings assigned are Reserve Pitchers — they appear in the Reserve Pitchers table below the lineup card (not in the main lineup) and can be dragged to reorder.
  • Any innings not covered by a locked pitcher are filled dynamically by the algorithm using flexible pitchers (availability > 0, no innings assigned). The algorithm will fail if it cannot find a valid pitcher for every inning.
🥅 Catching
  • Catcher Innings buttons (1–7) let you manually assign which innings each player catches (highlighted amber). There is no consecutive-innings requirement — assign any combination freely.
  • Every inning (1–7) must have exactly one catcher assigned. The app blocks generation if any inning is uncovered.
  • Two players cannot be assigned to catch the same inning — the app blocks this at selection time.
  • A player may catch as many innings as you assign. There is no per-player catching limit enforced by the app.
🏟️ Field Positions & Exclusions
  • Exactly 9 players must be on the field each inning. Any attending players beyond 9 rotate to the bench (OUT).
  • No position may be assigned to more than one player per inning.
  • Each active player must play at least 2 infield innings (P, C, 1B, 2B, 3B, or SS all count as infield).
  • No player may be assigned the same position for more than 4 innings (scaled up from 3 for the 7-inning game).
  • The Excluded Positions checkboxes exclude a player from specific positions (1B, 2B, 3B, SS, LF, CF, RF). The algorithm never assigns them to excluded positions, and those positions are hidden from their manual dropdowns on the lineup card. P and C also appear here, but those two positions are already fully controlled by the Pitcher/Catcher innings columns — checking them off is just a visual note and doesn't change the algorithm.
  • The Primary Positions toggles let you mark a player's best/preferred positions for your own reference. This is a visual note only — the algorithm does not currently use it when generating a lineup.
  • The app warns if a player is excluded from all outfield or all infield positions, and blocks generation if a player is excluded from every field position.
  • A more precise check also runs on the Roster Settings tab: it calculates whether each player has enough allowed positions to cover their required field innings without exceeding the 4-inning-per-position cap, and whether each position has enough eligible players roster-wide to cover all innings. If it finds a bottleneck, it blocks generation and names the specific player or position under "Cannot generate lineup," rather than letting you generate and hit a failure later. If generation still fails for a scheduling reason this pre-check can't see (like locked innings colliding with fair bench rotation in a specific inning), the app reports the real cause pulled directly from the failed attempts themselves — which inning, which position, and roughly how often it was the blocker.
📋 Bench & Attendance
  • Players marked as not attending are excluded from the lineup entirely and shown as "absent" on the lineup card.
  • A minimum of 6 attending players is required to generate a lineup (the app will run short-handed and leave the least essential positions empty). Glenview league rules require 8 players minimum to avoid a forfeit — see the League Rules section below.
  • Bench (OUT) innings are guaranteed to differ by at most 1 inning between any two active players — for example if the fair average works out to 1.75 innings per player, some players sit exactly 1 and the rest sit exactly 2, and nobody sits 0 or 3. This is a hard rule: the app will never sit a player extra just to force a lineup through. If it can't be satisfied (usually because of position exclusions), generation fails and you'll get a message listing up to 5 specific changes to try instead.
  • Players who are assigned as pitcher or catcher in a given inning are never benched in that same inning.
⭐ Defensive Rating & Biases
  • Each player can be assigned a Defensive Rating (1–10) on the roster tab, where 10 is the best defender. The default is 5. Ratings are color-coded: green (8–10), amber (5–7), red (1–4).
  • Ratings are for reference only unless one or more defensive biases are enabled via the checkboxes on the roster tab. Biases are independent and can be combined.
  • Bias: Better defenders to key positions — when assigning field positions, the algorithm fills the most demanding positions first (SS → CF → 2B → 3B → LF → RF → 1B) and tries higher-rated defenders in those slots first.
  • Bias: Poorer defenders sit out more often — when choosing who goes OUT each inning, lower-rated players are selected for bench duty first, while still keeping the overall bench rotation fair (no player sits excessively more than others). Can be combined with the late-innings bias below — both apply together if both are checked.
  • Bias: Poorer defenders sit out in later innings — the first half of the game prefers benching your better defenders (banking their one mandatory bench inning early, out of the way), which preserves your weaker defenders' bench time for the second half, when it matters most. Combined with Bias: Poorer defenders sit out more often (which decides who gets extra bench innings at all, via each player's exact quota — see Bench & Attendance below), these two together mean weaker defenders both sit more overall and specifically during the innings that count.
  • When either bench-out bias is enabled, defense rating decides who gets the extra bench inning when the total doesn't divide evenly across the roster (e.g., with 12 players and 7 innings, 9 players sit 2 innings and 3 sit 1 \u2014 the bias picks the lowest-rated defenders for the "sits 2" group). Defense rating also decides which inning each player sits in, front-loading or back-loading bench time for lower-rated players depending on which bias is checked. Every player's total bench time is still guaranteed to land within 1 inning of everyone else's \u2014 the bias controls who gets the shorter or longer end of that gap, not whether the gap exists.
💾 Saving & the Lineup Card
  • All roster settings (attendance, pitcher availability, assigned innings, catcher innings, position exclusions, defensive ratings, and bias preferences) are automatically saved in your browser and restored next time you open the app.
  • This saved data lives only in your current browser — it does not travel with the app file itself. To share your roster settings with someone else (or move it to another device), use Export to download a settings file, then send them that file along with the app. They open the app and use Import to load it in — it'll then be saved in their browser too.
  • The Reset to defaults button clears all saved settings and returns the roster to its original state.
  • On the lineup card, the batting order and reserve pitcher order can be reordered by dragging the ⠶ handle. Position dropdowns can be manually overridden for any player in any inning.
  • Each time you generate a lineup, the batting order is automatically set in three groups: the top 3 spots go to the best combined Hitting + Speed ratings, the next 3 go to the highest Power ratings, and the rest fill in by Hitting rating (best to worst). The power group is locked in first, so a player rated very high on Power will never also land in the top 3 spots. You can freely drag-and-drop to adjust it afterward — your manual order isn't overwritten until you generate again.
  • The Rule violations panel on the lineup card flags any issues with the current lineup (duplicate positions, infield inning minimums, pitching limits, etc.).
  • The Innings Sat Summary table lists every attending player and how many innings they're benched, sorted from most to fewest. It updates live if you manually change a position dropdown, and it's included on the downloaded PDF too.
  • Use Download PDF to print or save the lineup card. The PDF respects your current batting order and reserve pitcher order.
